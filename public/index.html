Zero-Knowledge Credential Verification

Prove identity, degrees, certifications, and qualification thresholds on the Midnight blockchain without disclosing raw names, scores, or private salts.

1. Issue Credential Commitment
The issuer authority computes a 32-byte cryptographic hash commitment off-chain and posts only the commitment hash to Midnight.
2. Zero-Knowledge Proof
The holder executes the Compact ZK circuit to prove on-chain validity without transmitting raw fields to the verifier or ledger.
